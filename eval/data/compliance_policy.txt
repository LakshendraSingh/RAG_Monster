AURORA SYSTEMS — DATA GOVERNANCE & COMPLIANCE POLICY (DOC-POL-09)

1. Encryption Standards
All customer data at rest in S3 buckets and RDS databases must be encrypted using AES-256-GCM with customer-managed KMS keys rotated every 365 days. All data in transit must use TLS 1.3 (TLS 1.2 is permitted only for legacy webhook integrations until June 2026).

2. Data Retention and Deletion
- Application logs: retained for 90 days in hot storage, then purged automatically.
- Audit and access logs: retained for 7 years in immutable WORM storage for SOC 2 Type II compliance.
- Customer PII deletion (GDPR / CCPA "Right to be Forgotten"): upon verified request, all PII must be hard-deleted from primary databases within 14 business days and purged from encrypted backups within 30 calendar days.

3. Compliance Audits
Aurora Systems undergoes an external SOC 2 Type II audit annually in October and an ISO 27001 surveillance audit every 12 months. Penetration testing is conducted semi-annually by an independent CREST-certified vendor.
