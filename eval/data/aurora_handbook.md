# Aurora Systems Employee Handbook (2025 Edition)

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave
Full-time employees at Aurora Systems receive 22 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.83 days per month. Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days may be carried over into the first quarter (until March 31) of the following year. Sick leave is separate from PTO and includes up to 10 paid sick days annually.

## 2. Remote Work and Core Hours
Aurora Systems operates on a hybrid-first model. Engineering and Product teams are expected to be in-office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, while Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays are remote-flexible. Core collaboration hours across all time zones are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time (PT). Employees working remotely for more than 14 consecutive days from outside their home state must notify People Operations at least 10 business days in advance for tax compliance.

## 3. Equipment and Expense Policy
Every new hire receives a one-time home office stipend of $1,200 USD during their first 30 days. Monthly internet and co-working reimbursement is capped at $150 USD per month. Expense reports exceeding $500 USD require pre-approval from a VP-level manager via the Expensify portal and must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date.

## 4. Security Incident Response
If an employee suspects a phishing attempt, lost laptop, or unauthorized data access, they must report it immediately to the `#sec-incidents` Slack channel or email `security@aurorasystems.io`. Critical (SEV-1) security incidents must be acknowledged by the on-call Security Engineer within 15 minutes and escalated to the CISO within 1 hour.
