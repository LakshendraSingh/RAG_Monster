# Aurora Systems Employee Handbook (2021 Archival Edition) - SUPERSEDED

> WARNING: This document is for historical reference only. It was officially superseded by the 2025 Edition on January 1, 2025.

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Historical Leave
Under the 2021 policy, full-time employees were entitled to 15 days of PTO per calendar year, accrued at 1.25 days per month. Carryover was strictly prohibited; all unused leave was forfeited on December 31. Sick leave was capped at 5 days annually.

## 2. In-Office Attendance
The 2021 policy required three mandatory in-office days per week: Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Remote work was restricted to Tuesdays and Thursdays with direct manager approval. Core hours were 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time.

## 3. Historical Equipment Allowances
The 2021 one-time home office equipment stipend was $500 USD. Internet reimbursement was capped at $50 USD per month. Any expense over $250 USD required Director-level approval.

## 4. Legacy Security Incident SLA
In 2021, SEV-1 security incidents were required to be acknowledged within 45 minutes and escalated to the VP of Infrastructure within 3 hours.
